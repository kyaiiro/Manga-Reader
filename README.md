### Things this app will have:
- ✅ Manga (duh)
- ✅ Saving entries to your home for quick access
- Custom cover art on saved entries
- Saving pages of manga locally
- 3 defaut sources, mangadex, weebcentral and dynasty scans
<br> ↳ Support for custom sources provided it's written in rust and the responses are given in a way my code will understand
- Discord RPC (Shows what you're reading and what chapter you're on)
- 2 page wide reading mode (setting)
- 2 way (reading on the app updates the web, updating the web updates progress on the app) tracking with Anilist and MAL (later, AL first)
- NO PAID FEATURES NOR ADS cause I hate those
- and more
